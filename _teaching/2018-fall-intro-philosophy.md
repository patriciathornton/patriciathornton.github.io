Instructor of record, Fall 2018 – Spring 2019.

An introduction to the major branches of philosophy through specific problems: Cartesian skepticism and normative epistemology, theories of personal identity, Kantian and utilitarian approaches to world poverty, social duties and immigration, the meaning of life in Tolstoy and Nagel, and a closing unit on Plato's *Republic*.

The personal identity unit begins with Christopher Nolan's *Memento* rather than a lecture. Students construct their own identity problems from the film before we turn to Locke and Parfit, so that the theories arrive as answers to puzzles they have already built themselves.
