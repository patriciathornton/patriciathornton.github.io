Instructor of record, Spring 2019. Undergraduate philosophy majors.

A survey of early modern philosophy that situates the canonical figures within the intellectual world they actually inhabited — including a unit on natural science and magic, where the boundary between the new science and the occult was still being drawn.

Students who might glaze over at rationalism versus empiricism become invested when the question is what distinguished Boyle's chemistry from alchemy, and why that distinction mattered philosophically.
